Blog Life as Fullstack Dev

As a Fullstack Website Developer, I am on both sides at once: the front-end (the viewable screens) and the back-end (the mechanics of the system). Here is what my working day looked like.

08:30 Daily Stand-up

The day started by viewing the tickets on Jira and a quick stand-up with the team to align priorities and discuss impediments.

10:00 Frontend Development

I created a responsive UI that was built using React and Tailwind CSS, designed to be consistent, fast, and easily accessible on different devices.

13:00 Backend & Database

Post-lunch, we moved on to Node.js & Express. We designed our REST API, PostgreSQL database, authentication, and data validation.

15:30 Code Review and Testing

I reviewed my team member's pull request, wrote the unit tests, and checked that the CI/CD pipeline passed before deploying the feature.

17:00 Evaluation & Learning

Days end by updating documentation, noting down progress, and spending time learning new technology.

Furthermore, being a full-stack developer means constantly adapting. The challenges are diverse, yet there is a unique sense of satisfaction in seeing a digital product that functions well and is used by many people.
